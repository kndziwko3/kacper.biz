## Direction contract

THESIS: kacper.biz is a dark product studio with one real object in it: a machined 96% keyboard of exactly 100 keys,
three of them solid copper, shot like a product and taken apart by the scroll; it is the tool one person uses to build
both companies. Refused: an abstract sculpture that means nothing, a floating blob, particles, a glowing globe.

OWN-WORLD: warm studio black #0E0C0A, graphite #191613, cream paper #EDE7DC for the evidence sheets, text #EDE6DA on
dark and ink #15120F on paper, one accent: copper #C8703F (the three copper caps, the primary action). The board:
cream PBT alphas, graphite PBT modifiers, copper Esc, K and Enter, anodised graphite case with a diamond-cut chamfer,
legends in Martian Mono. Funnel Display (headings, the name), Funnel Sans (text), Martian Mono (labels, data).
Controls are keycaps: 0.6rem radius, a darker skirt edge, 2 px of travel when pressed; copper cap = primary.

STORY: the visitor meets Kacper's name beside the board; the caps lift into a 10 x 10 grid, 97 PBT and 3 copper that
step forward (only 3 in 100 micro-firms list a website in CEIDG); the board types "Dzień dobry, Panie Tomaszu" by
itself, AltGr+N for the ń, while the page spells it letter by letter, then Enter (OutreachPilot: find, write in
Polish, send from your own mailbox); a cream sheet brings the sector index, the benchmark and the plans; the board
comes apart layer by layer for FastLanding (caps, switches, plate, PCB, case), then prices, process and real client
sites; it closes in on the copper Enter for Justyna's calendar and the enquiry form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed studio. Desktop: the board floats on the right in a steep three-quarter view, the numpad
corner bleeding off the frame, the copper caps catching the key light, soft shadow on the dark floor; "Kacper Rękawek"
in two lines at about 11vw on the left, one line of role under it. Header keys: "kacper.biz" left; right: EN, the copper
"Umów rozmowę" key (primary), Menu. The chapter rail is a row of keys on the bottom edge. Phone: the board in the top
half, the name at about 18vw below it.

FORM: canon, the category standard played straight (position 6 of 7 in the round, seed key 1d695112), craft bar
Oryzo. Signature interaction: the scroll-driven keyboard, one instanced set of 100 caps whose poses (board, grid,
typing, exploded build, close on Enter) blend on a damped chapter coordinate while the camera rides a rail, each
chapter with its own light and lens (Neutral tone map, depth of field); the caps under the mouse press. Motion grammar:
machined, weighted, damped; the object moves between chapters, the type holds still; one line-mask reveal per chapter
headline, and the greeting typed in step with the keys.
